# Design direction

## Concept

**Catatan lapangan pembelajaran**: an editorial field journal documenting a teacher's progression from guided instruction to independent practice, project development, and collaborative learning. The portfolio should feel grounded in a real school and a real teaching process, not like a SaaS dashboard or a generic collection of UI cards.

## Mood and dials

- **Mood:** observant, warm, thoughtful, and professional. Human evidence takes priority over decoration.
- **ENERGY:** restrained. Use emphasis to direct attention, not to make every section compete.
- **RHYTHM:** varied. Alternate narrative sections, timelines, document lists, video evidence, and short analytical groupings.
- **MOTION:** minimal. Interaction should be immediate and functional; respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Visual language

- **Palette:** deep blue-green ink `#20343d`, warm paper `#f5f1e8`, muted text `#586a6b`, terracotta `#92452a`, and quiet sage/mist accents. Small text uses the darker terracotta token, not a pale decorative orange.
- **Typography:** retain the local system sans-serif stack for reliable reading across devices. Use scale, spacing, weight, and editorial labels to establish hierarchy instead of adding an external font dependency.
- **Graphic motif:** a curved route with circular waypoints represents the progression of the practicum. Prefer paths, lines, circles, and documentary media over faux software windows and repeated square tiles.
- **Layout:** use open timelines, ruled evidence lists, and text-led analysis. Reserve contained surfaces for media players and a small number of intentional notes.
- **Media:** show actual teaching and student activity as evidence. Do not use decorative stock imagery or imply that a selected clip represents an entire meeting.

## Content and interaction

- Keep the two portfolio tracks easy to distinguish and preserve chronological reading order.
- Label evidence by period, meeting, and purpose. Distinguish a complete lesson recording from a curated compilation.
- Links and controls need clear hover, active, and keyboard-focus states, with touch targets of at least 44 px where practical.
- Avoid motion that adds no information. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Scope note

This direction governs the current portfolio draft. It does not imply that unpublished student projects, permissions, or missing assessment evidence have been resolved.
